**E-commerce Sales & Business Intelligence Analysis — SQL**

– Designed and analyzed a relational sales database containing sales, products, customers, stores, regions, time, and returns data for 1997–1998.

– Developed complex SQL queries using **JOINs, CTEs, aggregations, subqueries, and filtering** to answer business questions related to revenue, sales performance, customers, products, stores, regions, and returns.

– Built business KPIs including **Total Revenue, Total Units Sold, Average Order Value (AOV), Profit Margin, Return Rate, Revenue Share, and Revenue Lost**.

– Applied advanced **SQL Window Functions** including `RANK()`, `DENSE_RANK()`, `ROW_NUMBER()`, `LAG()`, `LEAD()`, `NTILE()`, `FIRST_VALUE()`, `CUME_DIST()`, and running totals to perform comparative and trend analysis.

– Performed **Year-over-Year, monthly, quarterly, customer-segment, product, regional, and store-level analysis** to identify sales trends and business performance patterns.

– Analyzed product profitability, customer purchasing behavior, loyalty-card performance, store efficiency, return rates, and revenue loss to support data-driven business insights.

– Created analytical queries for **Pareto analysis, cumulative revenue, moving averages, performance rankings, and contribution percentages**, demonstrating advanced SQL and business analytics capabilities.
